This was one of the most significant personal projects for me. It was an extremely intensive modeling course that I took back when I was still a complete beginner. This course was the biggest motivation for me to keep using 3D software.
It was created entirely in Blender 3D, following video tutorials by Chris Plush. It involved countless hours of learning and was a truly enjoyable experience.
